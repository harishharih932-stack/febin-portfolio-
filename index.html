About Me

The Story
Behind the
Edit

I'm Febin Lawrence — a freelance video editor who turns raw footage into stories that feel. When editing is done right, the viewer doesn't notice the cuts. They just feel the emotion.

With hands-on experience across YouTube content, brand films, social media reels, and ad creatives, I bring technical precision and creative instinct to every frame. My work is built on fast turnarounds, clear communication, and an obsession with pacing and rhythm.

From 15-second reels to long-form YouTube edits — every frame gets the same care.
